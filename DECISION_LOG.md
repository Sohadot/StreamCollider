# StreamCollider Decision Log

## DEC-001 — Semantic fixation
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding
- **Decision:** StreamCollider concerns mutually consequential interaction across independently governed computational domains.
- **Canonical thesis:** `StreamCollider studies what happens when independently governed compute streams become mutually consequential.`
- **Unit of analysis:** governance boundary.

## DEC-002 — Collision is not failure
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding
- **Decision:** Collision does not rename contention, backpressure, queueing, synchronization overhead, calibration drift, or other established phenomena.

## DEC-003 — Three-part admission rule
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding
- **Decision:** Admission requires all three: distinct governance, boundary crossing, and mutual consequence.

## DEC-004 — Vendor neutrality
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding
- **Decision:** No vendor, scheduler, quantum interface, interconnect, or reference architecture is the semantic center of StreamCollider.

## DEC-005 — First proving domain and expansion lock
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding
- **Decision:** First proving domain = HPC workload-management domain ↔ remote/independently governed quantum-resource domain.
- **Mandatory trigger:** `Reference Case 001 complete -> Gate 0 re-evaluation`.
- **Expansion lock:** No second proving domain before that trigger.

## DEC-006 — Unknowns are first-class output
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Provisional pending Reference Case 001
- **Decision:** Original value is tested by exposing unresolved authority, state, proof, responsibility, or recovery questions across independently governed boundaries.

## DEC-007 — Advanced-compute context horizon
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding contextual decision
- **Decision:** Long-horizon context includes advanced chips, chiplets, memory/compute fabrics, photonic/electronic integration, quantum–classical systems, and distributed heterogeneous compute.

## DEC-008 — Advanced-chip non-admission rule
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Binding
- **Decision:** A chiplet, UCIe link, memory interface, package fabric, accelerator boundary, or photonic link is not a Stream Collision by default.
- **Admission requirement:** identify who governs side A, who governs side B, what crosses the boundary, and what becomes mutually consequential.

## DEC-009 — Candidate second proving domain
- **Date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** Reserved, not authorized
- **Decision:** If Reference Case 001 passes re-evaluation, prefer an advanced multi-die/chiplet case with demonstrably distributed control or management authority as the second proving domain.
