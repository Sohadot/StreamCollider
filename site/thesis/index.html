Thesis

StreamCollider studies what happens when independently governed compute streams become mutually consequential.

Canonical definition

A Stream Collision occurs when computational streams governed by distinct control domains become mutually consequential across a governance boundary.

Three conditions

  1. Distinct governance.
  2. A real boundary crossing.
  3. Mutual consequence.

What collision does not mean

It does not rename contention, backpressure, queueing, synchronization overhead, calibration drift, faults or other established engineering phenomena.

Expansion lock

Quantum–classical integration is the first proving domain. Advanced chips are strategic context until a separate, evidence-backed case passes the same admission rule.
