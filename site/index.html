Admission Rule

A boundary alone is not enough.

A Stream Collision is admitted only when all three conditions are evidenced.

01

Distinct governance

Two domains hold materially different authority or control.

02

Boundary crossing

A stream, state, task, control action, measurement or request crosses or couples them.

03

Mutual consequence

Each side can materially alter what the other can execute, schedule, interpret, prove or recover.
